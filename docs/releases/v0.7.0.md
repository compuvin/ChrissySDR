# ChrissySDR 0.7.0

ChrissySDR 0.7.0 completes the safe SoapyRemote voice-transmit milestone for
licensed amateur-radio operators on a LAN. The operator tested transmit on the
FLEX-1500 in AM, NFM, USB, and LSB, and reported successful transmit tests on
the FLEX-6700. A separate FLEX-6700 low-input-voltage event stopped the radio;
it was not a ChrissySDR TX-bandwidth failure.

Since 0.7.0-beta.1:

- Added a quick local QSO log with callsign entry, viewing, deletion, and
  date-filtered ADIF export; saved radio profiles can also be deleted.
- Added capability-aware TX gain and TX antenna controls. Matching RX/TX
  antennas can be synchronized, or an advertised TX antenna selected separately.
- Added automatic mode-based TX hardware-bandwidth selection when the radio
  advertises adjustable choices. Fixed or unreported bandwidth is left alone.
- Prepared TX streams before key-up and improved transmit startup timing.
- Expanded TX failure tests and documented what the app can and cannot confirm
  when a radio or connection fails.
- Completed the 0.7 transmit checklist and two-radio testing.

Known issue: [Stop Audio can leave TX unavailable after RX restart](https://github.com/compuvin/ChrissySDR/issues/6).

Transmit only on frequencies and modes for which you are licensed, and verify
your station's RF output. The attached APK is debug-signed for direct testing;
it is not a production-signed or Google Play build.
